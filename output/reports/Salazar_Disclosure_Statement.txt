AI tools (GitHub Copilot, powered by Anthropic Claude) were used in the
following capacities during this research:

  (a) Code development: Assisted with writing Python scripts for data
      collection, statistical analysis, and figure generation. All code was
      reviewed, tested (59 automated tests), and validated by the author.

  (b) Statistical methodology: Consulted on implementation of structural break
      tests, bootstrap confidence intervals, and welfare analysis
      specifications. All methodological choices were validated against cited
      literature (Athey & Imbens, 2017; Amiti et al., 2019; Fajgelbaum et al.,
      2020).

  (c) Writing assistance: Assisted with drafting, editing, and formatting the
      manuscript, including LaTeX/PDF generation. All substantive claims,
      interpretations, and analytical judgments are the author's own.

  (d) Robustness and quality review: Assisted with code auditing, numerical
      verification, and narrative consistency checks.

All data collection, analytical decisions, parameter choices, and interpretive
conclusions are solely the responsibility of the author. AI tools were not used
to generate data or fabricate results.
